Turn Brotato into a grind!

- Replay waves to build your character.
- Replay costs twice the wave number in materials.
- Nightmare only.
- Half material drops.
- No Harvesting.
- Fixed 0% pickup range.
- No instant material attraction.
- Uncollected materials disappear at wave end.

For Brotato 1.2.0.1.
Source: https://github.com/danielsolin/brotato
